Frequently asked questions

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Email support@cloudnote.example.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever with no credit card required. Upgrade only when you need more storage or features.

Can I use CloudNote offline?

Absolutely. Pro and Team plans include full offline mode; your changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

How secure is my data?

Notes are end-to-end encrypted with AES-256 and stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers. Only you hold the keys.

Can I import notes from other apps?

Yes. Import from Markdown, plain text, HTML and popular note apps in a few clicks.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings at any time. You keep access until the end of your billing period and can export everything.
